\paragraph{Secrets}

  \item I received money from \Character{vampirehead} for my silence.
\end{itemize}


\paragraph{Actions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Offer help with the house and \Character{vampirehead}, you're a butler after all.
  
  \item If someone offers you money for something, accept.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Background}

I was a merchant before this family invited me into its household \timestamp{decades after the death of Christ}. I keep the house running, keep the accounts straight, and make sure everyone has what they need. I also know the value of discretion: when someone pays for silence, I give it to them.

Long ago, I saw that the flask handed to \Character{ghoul} at its turning was not the proper potion. I raised the matter privately with \Character{vampirehead}, who paid me to forget it. I did. At least, I forgot it for the price agreed.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 08:00}, I saw \Character{supremacist} leaving the cellar after tampering with the wine. It said it did not want to share a room with a mere human and paid me not to mention it. \timestamp{Yesterday, at 12:00}, \Character{usurper} paid me again to replace that wine with its own supposedly better bottle for \Character{newcomer}. I carried out both arrangements. Whatever happened after that is not my concern---unless someone makes it worth my while.

\paragraph{GM Notes}

\Character{buttler} is the key witness connecting the three apparent attempts on \Character{newcomer}: the old irregularity in \Character{ghoul}'s turning, \Character{supremacist}'s poisoned wine, and \Character{usurper}'s replacement bottle. It did not know the contents of either wine and did not intend the death. Let its greed make information costly, but ensure that pressure, a good bargain, or fear of being exposed can bring out the details of both payments.

}
