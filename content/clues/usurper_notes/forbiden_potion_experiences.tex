
\ClueSheet{clue:forbiddenpotion_exp_notes}{Forbidden potion experience notes}{

\paragraph{What it is}

One of \Character{usurper}'s notes.

It details results of experiments with the forbidden potion.

The original text is:

"
Resultados de experiencias com evoluao.

24 horas e o maximo para efeitos serem permanentes, a maioria das alteracoes ocorrem durante o sono.

Ingerir a pocao uma segunda vez resulta em morte quase instantanea.
"

But it is encrypted with Vigenere cipher with a repeated key: "sangue". The resulting text is:

"
Jefafxsdby xi wxcklmwnpouw uoz kpsdunu.

24 bsjaf k i qsxvsi tsrn kziatby mijez vyveaakhxws, n sumgrvg xek ayzyvscbkm suoexyq vueghxw o fuhs.

Antklmj a cuweg uzg miyuaju zwz ekmydtn kg qgrgk kyssr ohwlaazurwa.
"


\paragraph{What it reveals}

The main piece of information to take is that the forbidden potion is fatal when given twice.
