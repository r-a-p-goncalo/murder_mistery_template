\paragraph{Background}

\timestamp{In 400 AD}, I joined this family. I was a ruler who understood that order requires strength, hierarchy, and the will to use both. Vampires should stand above humans, not apologise to them. That belief has put me in constant conflict with \Character{traitor}, whose sympathy for humans is as nauseating as its loyalty to this family is doubtful.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 01:00}, I entered \Character{usurper}'s study and took a poison that kills humans but leaves vampires unharmed. \timestamp{Yesterday, at 08:00}, I put it in the cheap wine intended for \Character{newcomer}. I saw \Character{buttler} notice me as I left, so I paid it to keep quiet. I did not want to share a house with a human who thought it could rise above its place.

The wine I poisoned was supposed to settle the matter. If \Character{newcomer} died some other way, I want to know who interfered---and I will not let the family's weaklings use my contempt for humans as an excuse to condemn me.
