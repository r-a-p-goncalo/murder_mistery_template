\shortdescription{A compassionate reformer, preacher of morals.}

\ObjectivesHeading{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Find out what could kill the vampires (\PositiveReward{1}, \NegativeReward{1})
\end{itemize}

\LosingConditionsHeading{Losing conditions}
Fail to vote out \Character{newcomer}'s murderer, if there is one.

\SecretsHeading{Secrets}
\begin{itemize}
  \item I have been secretly corresponding with the \outsideentity{church}. I want vampires cured if possible and destroyed if they cannot be cured.

  \item Yesterday, I searched \Character{usurper}'s study for a way to kill vampires. I stole and used a ritual instead.
\end{itemize}

\ActionsHeading{Actions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Speak warmly about human dignity, mutual aid, and mercy. Offer practical help rather than abstract sympathy, especially when the party needs a volunteer.
\end{itemize}

\BackgroundHeading{Background}

Before I was a vampire, I spent my life helping people who had been ignored by those with wealth and power. I organized food, shelter, and medicine where I could. I believed that a good life was measured by how much safety one created for people with the least of it. Then I met \Character{lover}. He was strong without being cruel, and he made me believe that even someone born for violence could choose to use it well.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, \Character{lover} asked \Character{vampirehead} to let me join the family. The decision was controversial. \Character{supremacist} objected openly, which was normal for former dictator. \Character{vampirehead} accepted me because \Character{lover} would not give up. I thought immortality would give me centuries to do good.

I still remember that awful party, where \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{usurper}, \Character{enforcer}, \Character{buttler}, \Character{ghoul}, \Character{supremacist}, \Character{lover} and \Character{prevlover} talked about their lives. How they laughed while recounting stories with other's suffering.

I tried for a long time to change the family from within. I argued for restraint, protected the people I could reach, and begged the others to see humans as more than food or servants. Most laughed at me. Eventually, I began corresponding with the \outsideentity{church}. They seek a cure for vampirism. If there is no cure, I believe the vampires who prey on humans must be stopped permanently.

\Character{usurper} once joked, while drunk, that a vampire who receives some specific treatment dies, while telling me to kill myself. Damn it. What was it? I can't remember.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} introduced \Character{newcomer} at a family gathering. I could see that the head wanted to turn him, and I quietly warned him that immortality in this household came with a price. He listened politely, but did not seem interested in my opinions.

Over the last year, I have become uneasy about \Character{lover}. I have noticed him guarding messages and disappearing at odd times. I do not know whether he has discovered my contact with the \outsideentity{church}, or whether he is hiding something of his own. I still love him, it is a shame that the world would be better off without him.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, \Character{vampirehead} announced that \Character{newcomer} would join the family at a party on \timestamp{16/7/2026}. Every vampire was expected to attend.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks ago}, \Character{vampirehead} questioned if anyone else besides her, \Character{buttler} and \Character{newcomer} would want to help. She said that \Character{usurper} offered to help, and would reach the castle two nights before the party. From that public invitation, I knew \Character{usurper}'s study would be unattended the following morning.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 01:00}, I entered that study looking for the means to kill a vampire. I know that, at that time, I remembered what it was. I searched through its bottles and encrypted notes but could not find it. Instead, I took a ritual titled \"Make what is theirs someone else's\". I believed it would let me take the object later. I think there were others there, but I did not linger long enough to identify the other intruders.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 17:00}, I performed the ritual, hoping the means to kill the vampires would appear before me. I could no longer name the object I wanted or even state my goal clearly. Something happened, the ritual caught fire. But no object was presented to me. As the ritual was catching fire, it disappeared right in front of me. 

\timestamp{At 21:00}, I realized \Character{lover} was away from home. I did not follow him.

\GMNotes{

\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{traitor} is conspiring with the \outsideentity{church} and searched for a way to kill vampires. She has a clear reason to want a vampire dead.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

The ritual remains explain the scattered objects. The potion notes and the accounts of lunch trace the first dose to \Character{usurper}'s wine.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

She has a real plot against the family, which gives the players another possible explanation for the murder. Her ritual explains why the clues are scattered.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

She remembers \Character{usurper} mentioning a way to kill vampires. Solving her ritual remains also links the scattered clues to \Character{usurper}'s study.
