\paragraph{What it is}

A lot of instructions and notes about the \Clue{forbiddenpotion}.


"
This is an ungodly potion that forces a human to get further from god and nature. Everything shall hate a human that did this, such as the sun, time and death. In turn, the newly born vampire will be able to steal from nature, in the form of blood of its children.

It is a foolish errand to try to change this process, there is no good or bad, only change. Drinking this potion more than once will also not result in anything but the total destruction of the body and soul.

When the potion is first drank, the vampire that offered its blood may define rules that the newly created vampire will be forced to abide by, for life. It is simply not in the nature of \Character{vampirehead} to make use of this. That is not always the case, as we have in this family examples of mystical rules being used.
"
