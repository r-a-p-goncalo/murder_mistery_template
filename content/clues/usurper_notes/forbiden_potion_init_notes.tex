\paragraph{What it is}

One of \Character{usurper}'s notes.

It details initial notes regarding the forbidden potion.

The original text is:

"
Notas sobre evolucao.

O recetor da pocao ira pertencer a familia e adquirir os seus poderes e fraquezas.

O dador de sangue podera definir regras para o recetor. Poderia tornar o recetor um escravo por exemplo.
"

But it is encrypted with Caeser cipher offset 3.

"

The main piece of information to take is that the forbidden potion can be used to make a slave out of a new element of the family.
