\subsection{Relationship map}
\begin{itemize}

  \item \Character{vampirehead} is the original vampire and head of the family.

  \item \Character{traitor} is in cahoots with the \outsideentity{church} and hates vampires.

  \item \Character{traitor} pleaded \Character{newcomer} to abandon the idea of becoming a vampire.

  \item \Character{traitor} is trying to get a flask of the \Clue{forbiddenpotion} to give to all the vampires, hence killing them.

  \item \Character{usurper} is the main magic caster from the family.
  
  \item \Character{usurper} wants to become the new head. For this, it wants to create trouble for the other vampires, specially \Character{vampirehead}. While doing it, it wants to appear as good friends with everyone too.
  
  \item If \Character{vampirehead} leaves the position, the next in line is \Character{usurper}. This is revealed in \Clue{vampireranking}.

  \item  \Character{enforcer} is responsible for enforcing the rules, having a similar authority to the head. They are the only two supposed to have a full understanding of the family hierarchy.
  
  \item A vampire having a relationship with a human is against rules. This is specified in \Clue{rulebook}.
  
  \item When a family vote finds that a vampire broke a rule, the \Clue{rulebook} automatically strips that vampire's powers. The family may then exile or kill the powerless offender.
  
  \item The \Clue{rulebook} \Character{enforcer} uses is stored away behind \Clue{rocks}, using a password.
  
  \item \Character{ghoul} is treated as a pet animal, a jester. It wants revenge.
  
  \item \Character{ghoul} is a direct subordinate of \Character{enforcer}, and has to do its bidding. Only they are supposed to know this, as it gives the capability of \Character{enforcer} a chance to use it as a spy.
  
  \item \Character{vampirehead} wants to prepare the party with a day to spare. For that, it spends the previous day with \Character{newcomer}, \Character{usurper} and \Character{buttler}.
  
  \item \Character{usurper} reveals that it will leave its study to help \Character{vampirehead} prepare the party. This is done to give an opportunity to the other elements of the family to steal its magical tools. In this way, all family members will appear suspect.
  
  \item \Character{supremacist} is disgusted by \Character{newcomer}. It steals a poison that kills only mortals, then puts it in the cheap wine in the cellar.
  
  \item \Character{buttler} sees \Character{supremacist} tamper with the cheap wine. \Character{supremacist} pays \Character{buttler} to keep silent; it does not instruct \Character{buttler} to serve that wine.
  
  \item At lunch, \Character{usurper} pays \Character{buttler} to replace \Character{supremacist}'s poisoned wine with its own supposedly better wine. \Character{newcomer} drinks it, unknowingly receiving a first dose of the forbidden potion and becoming a vampire.

  \item \Character{usurper} knows that the potion turns a human only on the first dose; a further dose given to a vampire kills it. This is the mechanism behind the murder plan.
  
  \item \Character{ghoul} steals a ritual from \Character{usurper}.
  
  \item \Character{traitor} gets into the study, looking for an extra flask of the \Clue{forbiddenpotion}, but fails to find it.
  
  \item \Character{prevlover} steals a ritual that allows it to look into human's memories and alter them. It also allows it to wipe a vampire's memory. \Character{ghoul} notices this, and so is forced to tell \Character{enforcer}.
  
  \item \Character{ghoul} is hopeful that the ritual it stole is meant to free it from its shackles. Instead, the ritual allows it to look into the future, where it sees \Character{newcomer} die. This warning implies that \Character{newcomer} has already been turned. Forced to follow \Character{enforcer}'s standing order, \Character{ghoul} discloses the information.
  
  \item \Character{enforcer} now has to stop the murder. It asks for the help of \Character{prevlover}, telling it that it will ignore the sin of \Character{prevlover} of stealing if it lets go of the ritual and allows \Character{enforcer} to look into \Character{newcomer}'s memories.
  
  \item \Character{prevlover} wipes \Character{enforcer}'s memories.
  
  \item \Character{lover} and \Character{traitor} have been in a long-standing relationship; it is why \Character{traitor} was admitted to the family.

  \item \Character{lover} was having a secret relation with \Character{newcomer}.

  \item \Character{prevlover} had a secret relation with \Character{newcomer}, but \Character{newcomer} left \Character{prevlover} to be with \Character{lover}.

  \item \Character{newcomer} was using \Character{vampirehead} to gain immortality, powers, and to be able to be with \Character{lover}.
      
  \item \Character{supremacist} thinks that every human is inferior, and is trying to make every human into mindless cattle.
  
  \item \Character{traitor} and \Character{supremacist} have frequent fights regarding the value of vampires in the world throughout the years.
  
  \item At \Character{ghoul}'s turning, \Character{buttler} noticed that the flask was not the proper potion. \Character{vampirehead} paid \Character{buttler} for silence, giving the butler an older reason to fear exposure.

  \item \Character{buttler} is \Character{vampirehead}'s buttler. Everyone knows that \Character{buttler} will do anything for the right amount of money.

  \item \Character{vampirehead} wants to turn \Character{newcomer} into a vampire due to being in love with him, so she can confess and have a relationship without breaking rules.
  
  \item \Character{usurper} turns \Character{newcomer} into a vampire without its knowledge by arranging the first dose in the lunch wine.

  \item In the bedroom, \Character{vampirehead} gives \Character{newcomer} the correct turning potion, unaware that \Character{newcomer} has already received a first dose. The second dose kills \Character{newcomer}. \Character{vampirehead} leaves the bedroom and publicly says that the potion was given, making it the apparent killer while \Character{usurper} remains responsible for the plan.
  
  \item The main mystery is \Mystery{Who killed newcomer}.

\end{itemize}
