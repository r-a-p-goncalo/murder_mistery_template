  \item Speak warmly about human dignity, mutual aid, and mercy. Offer practical help rather than abstract sympathy, especially when the party needs a volunteer.

\BackgroundHeading{Background}

Before I was a vampire, I spent my life helping people who had been ignored by those with wealth and power. I organized food, shelter, and medicine where I could. I believed that a good life was measured by how much safety one created for people with the least of it. Then I met \Character{lover}. He was strong without being cruel, and he made me believe that even someone born for violence could choose to use it well.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, \Character{lover} asked \Character{vampirehead} to let me join the family. The decision was controversial. \Character{supremacist} objected openly, and \Character{enforcer} did not trust a human who kept talking about mercy. \Character{vampirehead} accepted me because \Character{lover} would not give up. I thought immortality would give me centuries to do good. Instead, it gave me centuries to watch this family feed on humans and call it tradition.

I still remember that awful party, where \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{usurper}, \Character{enforcer}, \Character{buttler}, \Character{ghoul}, \Character{supremacist}, \Character{lover} and \Character{prevlover} talked about their lives. How they laughed with other's suffering.

I tried for a long time to change the family from within. I argued for restraint, protected the people I could reach, and begged the others to see humans as more than food or servants. Most laughed at me. Eventually, I began corresponding with the \outsideentity{church}. They seek a cure for vampirism. If there is no cure, I believe the vampires who prey on humans must be stopped permanently. I know what that makes me in the eyes of this family. I would rather be called a traitor than keep pretending that cruelty is loyalty.

\Character{usurper} once joked, while drunk, that a vampire who receives some specific treatment dies, while telling me to kill myself. Damn it. What was it? I can't remember.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} introduced \Character{newcomer} at a family gathering. I could see that the head wanted to turn him, and I quietly warned him that immortality in this household came with a price. He listened politely, but power and belonging had their own appeal.

Over the last year, I have become uneasy about \Character{lover}. I have noticed him guarding messages and disappearing at odd times. I do not know whether he has discovered my contact with the \outsideentity{church}, or whether he is hiding something of his own. I still love him, which makes every uncertainty feel like a threat.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, \Character{vampirehead} announced that \Character{newcomer} would join the family at a party on \timestamp{16/7/2026}. Every vampire was expected to attend. A couple of weeks before the party, \Character{usurper} offered to help prepare the celebration. \Character{vampirehead} then asked whether anyone besides \Character{usurper}, \Character{buttler}, and \Character{newcomer} wanted to arrive two nights early to help. I declined. Like everyone else, I understood that the preparations would create an opportunity to enter \Character{usurper}'s study.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 01:00}, I entered that study looking for the potion that could kill a vampire. I searched through its bottles and encrypted notes but could not find it. Instead, I took a ritual titled \textit{Make what is theirs someone else's}. I believed it would let me take the potion later. I did not linger long enough to identify the other intruders in the study.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 17:00}, after a strange fog of missing memories had settled over the family, I performed the ritual. I could no longer name the bottle I wanted or even state my goal clearly. It seemed like it was not going to work, until it disappeared from sight. There should have been leftovers, but no, nothing.

\timestamp{At 21:00}, I realized \Character{lover} was away from home. I did not follow him.

\GMNotes{

\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{traitor} is secretly working with the \outsideentity{church}, openly condemns the family, searched for the means to kill vampires, and performed a ritual that scattered evidence from \Character{usurper}'s study. Her motive is real, and the intercepted correspondence can expose it. She is therefore a strong and intentional red herring.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

\Character{traitor} did not find the forbidden potion, did not handle the wine at lunch, and performed her ritual at \timestamp{17:00}, five hours after \Character{newcomer} had already received the hidden first dose. Her ritual explains why study items and clues are spread around the castle, but it did not administer the dose that made the official turning fatal.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

\Character{traitor} supplies a morally serious alternative suspect rather than a simple villain. Her player can force the group to confront the family's treatment of humans while deciding how much of a genuine conspiracy to disclose. Her failed ritual is also the in-world explanation for the clues being scattered beyond \Character{usurper}'s study.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

When the stealing-ritual remains are solved, \Character{traitor} can admit that the scattered potions and papers originated in \Character{usurper}'s study. That fact encourages the group to trace the important bottles back to their owner. Combined with \Character{buttler}'s account that \Character{usurper} replaced the wine at lunch, it turns the apparent evidence of \Character{traitor}'s crime into a route toward \Character{usurper}'s murder plan.
