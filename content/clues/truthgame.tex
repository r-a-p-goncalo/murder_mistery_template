

\ClueSheet{truthgame}{Truth game}{

\paragraph{What it is}

A game where players will have to compete by drinking.

Each person that wants to play has to take a shot, whoever doesn't loses the right to play the game. This continues in turns, until someone drinks the last shot alone.

This person reads the statements of the game aloud and says which is true and which is false. In the end, the GM tells the person in secret how many it got correct. Then, the game can't be played anymore.

The game has these statements:

The \Clue{forbiddenpotion} given to \Character{newcomer} was actually poison. (false)

\Clue{newcomer} had a relationship with a vampire. (true)

\Clue{newcomer} was killed on purpose. (true)
