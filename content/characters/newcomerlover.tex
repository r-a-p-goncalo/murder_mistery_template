\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{In 700 AD}, I was a warrior who had spent a mortal life protecting people who could not protect themselves. \Character{vampirehead} saw a use for that strength and brought me into her family as its protector. I accepted because immortality promised the means to keep fighting forever. At my turning, \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{usurper}, \Character{enforcer}, \Character{buttler}, \Character{ghoul} and \Character{supremacist} were already family. It was a somewhat cool party.

\timestamp{In 1000 AD}, \Character{prevlover} joined as the family's investigator.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, I asked \Character{vampirehead} to admit \Character{traitor}. I loved her, and I believed that her habit of helping ordinary people would make our family less cruel. The others saw a charitable human who did not belong among vampires, especially \Character{supremacist}. I argued until \Character{vampirehead} accepted her.

Over the centuries, \Character{traitor}'s concern for humans became sharper and more secretive. I have noticed unexplained journeys and carefully hidden correspondence. I fear she is entangled in something dangerous. I have not asked directly because I still love her, and because I am afraid of the answer.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} began bringing \Character{newcomer} to family gatherings I met him as the head's favored guest, and at first I kept my distance. Then we talked. A year ago, friendship became a secret relationship. He was human, I was a vampire, and the family's rules made that enough to ruin us both if it became known.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, \Character{vampirehead} announced that \Character{newcomer} would join the family at a party on \timestamp{16/7/2026}. Every vampire was expected to attend.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks before the party}, \Character{vampirehead} questioned if anyone else besides her, \Character{buttler} and \Character{newcomer} would want to help. She said that \Character{usurper} offered to help, and would reach the castle two nights before the party.

\timestamp{Since 16:00 of yesterday}, I've noticed problems with my memory. It is not simply that I forgot stuff, I'm also having a hard time maintaining new memories.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 21:00}, I called \Character{newcomer} and asked him to meet me outside. He told me our relationship had to end. He believed that, once \Character{vampirehead} turned him, the head would expect them both to have a romantic relationship. He said that perhaps there could be another time for us. We spent one final night together instead.

\timestamp{This morning, at 11:00}, I begged \Character{newcomer} not to end things so abruptly. We spoke outside, where I thought no one could hear us. I think someone saw us, but the details are blury. I can't quite remember it for some reason.

\GMNotes{

\Character{lover} was secretly involved with \Character{newcomer}, who wanted to end the relationship. He also paid \Character{buttler} to keep quiet about their last conversation.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

The potion notes and the accounts of lunch point to \Character{usurper}'s wine as the first dose. \Character{lover} was not at that lunch and did not handle the wine.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

He has a relationship to hide and a reason to find the killer. His concern for \Character{traitor} also connects him to her secrets.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

His account explains \Character{newcomer}'s missing night. If the payment is uncovered, it also gives the players a reason to question \Character{buttler}.
