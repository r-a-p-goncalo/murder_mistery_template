\CharacterSheet{vampirehead}{Head Vampire}{

\shortdescription{The ancient founder and imperious head of the family.}

\paragraph{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Find out why the newcomer was killed.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Secrets}
\begin{itemize}
  \item I wanted to turn \Character{newcomer} into a vampire because I was in love with him.
\end{itemize}


\paragraph{Actions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Be arrogant, give orders, you are the vampire head after all.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Background}

\timestamp{Thousands of years ago}, I was human and desperate to escape every human weakness: hunger, pain, and dependence on other people. Something heard that desire and made me the first vampire. Freedom did not cure my loneliness, so I gathered a family around me and became its head.

\timestamp{Three thousand years ago}, I welcomed \Character{usurper}, whose blood potion could turn willing humans into vampires. Its hunger for magic has troubled me for centuries, but it has repeatedly made itself useful. I made \Character{enforcer} my second authority and created the \Clue{rulebook} so the family could punish those who break our laws. I also accepted \Character{usurper}'s communication tattoos for new members, believing they would keep the family connected.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, I met \Character{newcomer}. I wanted it close, but I kept my distance because the rules forbid a vampire from loving a human. If I turn it \timestamp{tonight}, I can finally confess without breaking the laws I made. \timestamp{Yesterday}, I prepared the party with \Character{newcomer}, \Character{usurper}, and \Character{buttler}; \timestamp{today}, I intend to welcome \Character{newcomer} properly into my family.

\paragraph{GM Notes}

\Character{vampirehead} is the apparent killer because it physically gives \Character{newcomer} the second potion, but it uses the correct ritual and does not know about the first dose. Its concealed love for \Character{newcomer}, its authority over the rules, and its past payment to \Character{buttler} all make it a convincing suspect. Once the double-dose effect is established, make clear that the head was deceived and that the person who arranged the lunch wine is responsible.

}
