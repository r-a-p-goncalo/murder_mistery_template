

\ClueSheet{clue:memritualleftovers}{Memory ritual leftovers}{

\paragraph{What it is}

Ritual leftovers with the symbol of memory. When solved, it shows a vision of \Character{prevlover}, in panic, and then of \Character{enforcer}.


\paragraph{How can it be found / solved}

The first player to correctly point out what the symbol is, receives visions, given by the GM.

\paragraph{What it reveals}

Points to \Character{prevlover} having wiped \Character{enforcer}'s memory, resulting in the loss of memory for all the vampires.

All the ritual clues, when discovered, motivate the affected characters to reveal what happened in \Character{usurper}'s study.
