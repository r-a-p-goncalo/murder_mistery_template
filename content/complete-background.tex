\subsection{Complete background}

This is the GM-only account of what actually happened. It distinguishes the true chain of events from the suspicions and partial information available to the players.

\paragraph{Long before the party}

\Character{vampirehead} was once, \timestamp{thousands of years ago}, a simple human. One day, while looking at the moon and the stars, dreaming of freedom, that changed. Freedom from hunger, freedom to cruise the skies, freedom to go everywhere, freedom from the pain of dealing with other humans, \Character{vampirehead} desired detachment from humanity, and something heard. From that moment on, \Character{vampirehead} was a vampire, free from all, but hated by all. The world hated with such strength that the sun threatens her existence.

\Character{vampirehead} grew lonely, and then lonelier, and then even lonelier. Until the decision to start a family appeared.

One day, \timestamp{3 thousand years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} met \Character{usurper}, a human with a strong interest in magic, and a strong desire for power. \Character{usurper} devised a potion. A potion created with the blood of a vampire, that, when given to a human with the desire to share the same benefits, will turn that human into family. \Character{vampirehead} could add more terms to the contract, but that did not seem to be an interest.

\Character{usurper} knows that, if this potion is given to a vampire, it will kill it.

Everything seemed well for \timestamp{a thousand years}, with a lot of fun things that two vampires could do. But \Character{vampirehead} was displeased of \Character{usurper}'s thirst for power. \Character{usurper} seemed like a good family member, but it was always obvious that the priority lied in the mystic arts.

This was shown how the research took over \Character{usurper}'s life. There was always a mess in the house, artifacts spread, things disappearing. For \Character{usurper}, it was nice that it was hard for \Character{vampirehead} to spy on what research was being done, as chaos was not pleasing to the head.

\timestamp{2 thousand years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} introduced \Character{enforcer} to the family as someone that should enforce rules. Someone whose word would be second only to \Character{vampirehead} when following the rules that \Character{vampirehead} devised then and there, written into a book, \Clue{rulebook}.

This book gained magical properties, making it so that, if a vote in the family shows that a rule as been broken, the vampire that broke the rule will automatically be stripped of its powers. This showed \Character{usurper} how powerful an original vampire is, that can create powerful artifacts almost by accident.

Later \Character{usurper} found out that, if \Character{vampirehead} was stripped of its powers, if the family had a ranking for inheritance of position, those powers would be received by the next in line. For that, \Character{usurper} proposed, in the middle of a million other documents, \Clue{vampireranking}, which \Character{vampirehead} accepted without paying much attention. Now, if the head falls, \Character{usurper} will receive its powers. The scheming had begun.


\Character{usurper} devised multiple magical tattoos. Two relevant were the tattoo that allowed the drawer to listen to the tattooed, and the other that allowed the tattooed to communicate with the target with the parent tattoo. \Character{usurper} presented this to \Character{vampirehead} as only the second tattoo. The claimed purpose is genuine, but the tattoo also allows the Usurper to eavesdrop when those messages are used.

\timestamp{Decades after the death of Christ}, what the family now needed was better house and money management, and so \Character{buttler}, a merchant known at the time for both its competence and greed, was invited.

\timestamp{At 100 AD.}, \Character{enforcer} met, while traveling, a charming jester. It was an artistic and charismatic soul, for which \Character{enforcer} fell. As vampires can't have relations with humans due to the rules, \Character{enforcer} asked \Character{vampirehead} to turn \Character{ghoul}, the jester, into a vampire.

But \Character{enforcer} wanted more, it wanted someone that would certainly follow its rules. And so, it asked \Character{vampirehead} that the potion was given it \Character{enforcer}'s blood instead. In this way, \Character{enforcer} could add a rule to the contract, using the rule that ''\Character{ghoul} will always follow \Character{enforcer}'s instructions, when given as orders''.

At the turning ceremony, \Character{buttler} noticed that the flask that was given to \Character{ghoul} did not have the potion. When this was bought up in private to \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{vampirehead} paid for \Character{buttler}'s silence. 

As the years went by, several new family members were added.

\Character{lover} was a strong and manly warrior that would serve as the protector of the family. It was added \timestamp{at 200 AD}.

\Character{supremacist} was a terrible dictator that treated the vampires well. It was added \timestamp{at 400 AD}.

\Character{prevlover} was an interesting investigator, and a useful member for the household when mysteries occurred. It was added \timestamp{at 1000 AD}.

\Character{lover} and \Character{traitor} were in love. And so \Character{traitor} was added to the family, even if it was a bit controversial. After all, \Character{traitor} was a good Samaritan that went around doing good deeds. The thought process was that being immortal would allow for more good deeds, but obviously that was a stretch. \Character{traitor} was added \timestamp{at 1400 AD}.

At some point, \Character{traitor} took issue with the nature of its new family, starting conniving with the \outsideentity{church}. For \Character{traitor}, Vampires should be cured, or better, destroyed. While humans, specially the poor from outside countries, should be taken in.

This contrasts specially with \Character{supremacist}, which, over the years, thinks more and more that humans should be all killed or turned into cattle. \Character{supremacist} hates specially poor humans, with no authority, going into countries that are not theirs. The good times were when kings ruled.

Throughout the years, \Character{usurper} offers help to any vampire that needs it, and comes to be kwown as the cute helper.

\paragraph{During this last 2 years}

\timestamp{2 years ago}, \Character{newcomer} and \Character{vampirehead} came to know each other. \Character{vampirehead} is getting lonelier, and so invited \Character{newcomer} as guest to a party, where \Character{newcomer} met the other vampires for the first time.

Everyone knew that \Character{vampirehead} was courting \Character{newcomer}, and that it was a matter of time for \Character{newcomer} to join the family.

But, behind the scenes, \Character{newcomer} and \Character{prevlover} started a romantic relationship.

\timestamp{A year ago}, \Character{newcomer} and \Character{lover} started having a relationship too. And so, \Character{newcomer} left \Character{prevlover}, saying that ''What we're doing is wrong''.

As these affairs happens, \Character{vampirehead} is oblivious, making an effort to keep distance from \Character{newcomer} as to not break the rules.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks before}, \Character{usurper} asked if \Character{vampirehead} would want help with preparing the party. \Character{vampirehead} appreciated it, also asking if any other vampire besides \Character{usurper}, \Character{buttler} and \Character{newcomer} would want to go the day before to help prepare. Everyone refused, and some took notice that \Character{usurper} would not be in the study, which meant a good opportunity to steal artifacts.

\paragraph{The day before the party}

\timestamp{At 01:00m}, multiple vampires reach \Character{usurper}'s study.

\Character{ghoul} goes, wanting to look for a way to attain freedom from \Character{enforcer}. \Character{ghoul} finds a ritual titled ''To change the future'', stealing that.

\Character{prevlover} goes, wanting to look for a way to be together with \Character{newcomer} again. Some artifacts only work on humans. It finds a ritual titled ''To change and know a person'', stealing that. \Character{ghoul} notices this.

\Character{traitor} goes, looking for the forbidden potion, but fails to find it.

\Character{supremacist} goes and steals a poison that only works on humans.

\timestamp{At 8h00m}, \Character{supremacist} later poisons the wine. \Character{buttler} notices \Character{supremacist} as it was leaving. \Character{supremacist} tells \Character{buttler} that was not in the mood to share a space with a mere human and pays \Character{buttler} for its silence. 

\Character{vampirehead}, \Character{newcomer}, \Character{buttler} and \Character{usurper} have lunch together at \timestamp{12H00m}. \Character{usurper} had paid \Character{buttler} to switch the wine (that was poisoned), with a wine of its own, a wine "better". That wine had the forbidden potion.

\timestamp{At 15h00m}, Ghoul executes the ritual, looking into the future. It sees that \Character{newcomer} will die, not that that deserves much concern. Nevertheless, due to a previous order of \Character{enforcer}, \Character{ghoul} has to disclose all information it knows about the family. \Character{ghoul} did not immediately notice this, but this was showing that \Character{newcomer} had already turned.

\Character{enforcer} wants to prevent any problems, but does not want to disclose to \Character{vampirehead} that people were stealing \Character{usurper}. \Character{enforcer} calls \Character{prevlover}, and says that it will ignore its sin of stealing if it gives \Character{enforcer} the ritual. \Character{enforcer} wants to use it on \Character{newcomer}, to help finding clues to why \Character{newcomer} would be killed.

\Character{enforcer} panics and wipes \Character{enforcer}'s memory. \Character{enforcer} is left with a sense that something is wrong, but no longer remembers the warning, the ritual, or the agreement.
