\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{Decades after the death of Christ}, I was a merchant with a reputation for keeping clean books and making difficult arrangements seem effortless. \Character{vampirehead} invited me into her family because she needed my management skills. I accepted because immortality is the finest investment I have ever made. I got a party with only \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{enforcer} and \Character{usurper}. It was horrible, they truly needed me.

Since then, I have kept this castle supplied, orderly, and solvent.

\timestamp{In 100 AD}, a new member was introduced, \Character{ghoul}, an entertainer of sorts. He was presented by \Character{enforcer}, which seemed to desire a deeper relationship with him. At the party I did notice that the "forbidden potion" \Character{vampirehead} offered seemed to have a different hue. When I asked her, she offered me money for my silence, and so I did not press the subject further. 

Over the following centuries, the family grew. Every new arrival brought another room to prepare, another set of habits to accommodate, and another source of friction that someone had to smooth over before dinner.

\timestamp{In 500 AD}, \Character{supremacist} arrived. She had been a ruler, and she expected the house to recognise it. She was demanding company, but she understood ceremony and treated the family with more respect than most humans ever had. When \Character{vampirehead} gave her the task of judging possible additions to the family, I learned to keep my opinions about prospective guests to myself.

\timestamp{In 700 AD}, \Character{lover} joined as the family's protector. He was a wandering warrior, built for direct solutions and not especially interested in the household arrangements behind them. Still, he was courteous to me, and it is useful to have someone who can end an argument before it becomes expensive.

\timestamp{In 1000 AD}, \Character{prevlover} became our investigator. He had the habit of noticing details that other people expected to stay private, which made him an inconvenient guest but a useful family member.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, \Character{traitor} was admitted after \Character{lover} argued for her. She was known for helping people, which made her an unusual fit for this family from the beginning.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} introduced a human to the family at a party, \Character{newcomer}. He was a good help with the after-party chores, a nice change of pace.


\timestamp{Two months ago}, In the usual fashion,\Character{vampirehead} marked \timestamp{16/7/2026} as the date of the party to add \Character{newcomer} to the family, and all vampires, as was usual in the family, had to come. 


\timestamp{A couple of weeks ago}, \Character{vampirehead} questioned if anyone else besides her, me and \Character{newcomer} would want to help. She said that \Character{usurper} offered to help, and would reach the castle two nights before the party. The final list was \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{newcomer}, \Character{usurper} and, as is habit, me.


\timestamp{Two days before the party, at night}, \Character{usurper} and \Character{newcomer} arrived at the castle to help prepare. \Character{vampirehead} and I were already there.

\timestamp{Yesterday morning}, the cellar inventory and my accounts both acquired details I cannot explain. There is an unsigned payment, and a bottle had been set aside for \Character{newcomer}, but the visitor, the request, and the purpose of the payment have vanished from my mind. I hate an incomplete ledger.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 12:00}, I joined \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{newcomer}, and \Character{usurper} for lunch. I was about to serve the wine in question when \Character{usurper} intervened. She said she had brought a better bottle for each of us, and she was right. \Character{usurper} always comes to the rescue. As I did not really remember why there was a wine set aside, I let it go.

\timestamp{This morning, at 11:00}, I saw someone speaking with \Character{newcomer} in private outside. I know that I accepted money for my silence, but I can't remember any more details.

\GMNotes{

\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{buttler} manages the house and handled the service at lunch. She has a known habit of selling silence, possesses unexplained payments, and now holds a fresh payment from \Character{lover}. Those facts make it easy to imagine her concealing a crime or helping someone else conceal one.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

The lunch timeline clears \Character{buttler} of arranging the fatal dose. She was preparing to serve the wine already set aside when \Character{usurper} publicly replaced it with her own bottle for \Character{newcomer}. \Character{buttler} did not choose that bottle or know what it contained.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

\Character{buttler} is a practical, negotiable witness. She turns domestic details into leads: which bottle was meant to be served, who changed the plan, which guest was where, and which private conversation might supply a motive.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

If questioned about lunch, \Character{buttler} can establish the decisive sequence: a different wine had been prepared for \Character{newcomer}, then \Character{usurper} substituted her own supposedly superior bottle and \Character{newcomer} drank it. Combined with the evidence that a second turning dose kills a vampire, that account directs the investigation to \Character{usurper}'s wine as the first dose and to her as the murderer.
