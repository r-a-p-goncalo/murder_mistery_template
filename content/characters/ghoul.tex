\CharacterSheet{ghoul}{Ghoul}{

\shortdescription{The family's jester, bound to the Enforcer's commands.}

\ObjectivesHeading{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item End the game with \Character{enforcer} voted out. (\PositiveReward{1}, \NegativeReward{1})
\end{itemize}

\LosingConditionsHeading{Losing conditions}
Fail to see \Character{newcomer}'s murderer voted out, if there is one. You do not lose if \Character{enforcer} is voted out, even if she did not murder \Character{newcomer}.

\SecretsHeading{Secrets}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \Character{enforcer} had me turned with her blood. I must obey any clear order she gives me. (\NegativeReward{1}, \PositiveReward{1})
  
  \item I stole a ritual from \Character{usurper}'s study to find a way out of that bond. (\NegativeReward{1}, \PositiveReward{1})
  
  \item I stole a page from the \Clue{rulebook} because I could not bear to see my place in the hierarchy written down. (\NegativeReward{1}, \PositiveReward{1})
\end{itemize}

\ActionsHeading{Actions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Be theatrical, mischievous, and genuinely funny. Use jokes to make people underestimate how closely you listen.
  \item Needle \Character{enforcer} whenever it is safe, but do not knowingly disobey a clear order from her.
  \item When an order forces you to reveal something, make the family work to understand what you say without lying.
\end{itemize}

\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{In 100 AD}, I was a travelling jester. I could make a crowded room laugh, and I knew how to survive the mood of any king, priest, or drunk with a purse. While travelling, I met \Character{enforcer}. She loved my performances, and I loved her. Our only obstacle was that she was from a family of vampires.

She told \Character{vampirehead} that she wanted me in the family. I thought immortality would mean an endless road, endless audiences, and no master at all. Instead, \Character{enforcer} asked that my potion be made with her blood. She said it would bring us closer. The real turning happened in private, with only the two of us present. At the public celebration afterwards, \Character{vampirehead} handed me a harmless flask so that the family would believe the ceremony had happened then.

Only after I had changed did \Character{enforcer} explain the condition she had placed on the potion. When she gives me a clear order, I must obey. She soon gave me another order that has never expired: I must report any relevant information I learn about every other member of the family.

Over the centuries, \Character{enforcer} was oftentimes occupied with rogue new family members. I think there were coups, genocides, things like that.

Since then, members were added less frequently. Me and \Character{enforcer} spent more time together, which we spent mainly to create music. It was good, yes. But it was always frustrating how \Character{enforcer} could just trample on my opinion with her orders.

\timestamp{In 500 AD}, \Character{supremacist} arrived as the head's harsh judge of possible new members.

\timestamp{In 700 AD}, \Character{lover} came as the family's protector.

\timestamp{In 1000 AD}, \Character{prevlover} joined as the family's investigator.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, \Character{traitor} joined because \Character{lover} insisted that she belonged with us, even if she was almost certain to become a traitor of the family due to her "good" character.

\timestamp{About a year ago}, I stole a page from the \Clue{rulebook}. The page described the family hierarchy, including how I'm not directly connected to \Character{vampirehead}, but to \Character{enforcer}. I hid it because I was ashamed, yes, but that is not all. I don't love her anymore. I can't with the control she has over me. I dream of her death, of me being free again. But well, it is not like I'm going to show that. I'll keep on making the rest of the family smiling either way.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} began bringing \Character{newcomer} to family gatherings. Everyone could see that she intended to turn him eventually.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, In the usual fashion, \Character{vampirehead} marked \timestamp{16/7/2026} as the date of the party to add \Character{newcomer} to the family, and all vampires, as was usual in the family, had to come.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks ago}, \Character{vampirehead} questioned if anyone else besides her, \Character{buttler} and \Character{newcomer} would want to help. She said that \Character{usurper} offered to help, and would reach the castle two nights before the party. From that public invitation, I knew \Character{usurper}'s study would be unattended the following morning.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 01:00}, I entered that study looking for a way to free myself. I found and stole a ritual titled... Well, I actually can't remember the name. \Character{prevlover} arrived while I was there, also searching for a something. He saw me, dropped something in his hurry, and left with some object of the study.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 15:00}, I performed the ritual. It showed me \Character{newcomer} dead. I wish it was \Character{enforcer}'s death. The ritual left behind some weird  fiery leftovers. Great. I had no results and something to clean now. I would have kept the vision to myself if I could, but \Character{enforcer}'s standing order compelled me to tell her what I had seen, and so I did. \Character{prevlover}'s presence in the study was something I had also to report. 

\timestamp{Yesterday, sometime after 16:00}, a wave of confusion washes over me. I think that was the moment I lost some key memories, as I remember being able to recall them when warning \Character{enforcer}.

\timestamp{At 18:00}, I noticed the leftovers of the ritual were missing. One less problem I guess.


\GMNotes{

\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{ghoul} stole a page from the \Clue{rulebook}, entered \Character{usurper}'s study without permission, and used a stolen ritual that predicted \Character{newcomer}'s death. His resentment of \Character{enforcer} and desire to escape the bond give him a credible reason to create chaos or conceal information.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

The ritual was performed at \timestamp{15:00}, three hours after \Character{usurper}'s wine was served at lunch. \Character{ghoul}'s vision predicted the death but did not cause it. \Character{enforcer} can confirm that \Character{ghoul} reported the vision immediately because it was bound by a standing order.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

\Character{ghoul} is a reluctant witness to the stolen rituals and to \Character{prevlover}'s presence in the study. His binding gives \Character{enforcer} a way to force testimony, while his resentment gives the player a reason to make that testimony awkward, partial in tone, and difficult for the family to trust.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

An explicit order from \Character{enforcer} can make \Character{ghoul} repeat the warning about \Character{newcomer}'s death and place it at \timestamp{15:00}. That timing shows the death was already set in motion after lunch, directing attention to the bottle \Character{usurper} supplied rather than to the later memory ritual or the stolen future ritual.
