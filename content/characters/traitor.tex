\CharacterSheet{traitor}{Traitor}{

\shortdescription{A charitable idealist, preacher of the good.}

\paragraph{Objectives}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Find who killed the newcomer.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Secrets}
\begin{itemize}
  \item I want to kill all the vampires. 
\end{itemize}


\paragraph{Actions}
\begin{itemize}
  
  \item Spread your political views: Humans, specially the poor and unfortunate, should be treated well by the high society. Everyone should aspire to do good all the time.
  
  \item Tell people how they could help the party.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Background}

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, I joined this family because \Character{lover} and I were in love. Before that, I spent my life helping people, and I believed immortality would let me do even more good. Instead, I have watched this family prey on humans and excuse cruelty as tradition.

Over time, I began working with the \outsideentity{church}. I want vampires cured if they can be cured and destroyed if they cannot. \Character{supremacist} and I have fought about the value of human life for years, and I pleaded with \Character{newcomer} to abandon its plan to become one of us before it was too late.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 01:00}, while \Character{usurper}'s study was unattended, I searched for an extra flask of the \Clue{forbiddenpotion}. I wanted it so I could destroy this family before it harmed anyone else. I did not find it. I did not kill \Character{newcomer}, but I know my secret mission will make me an easy suspect.

\paragraph{GM Notes}

\Character{traitor} wants every vampire dead and searched for the fatal potion, so its church connection is a deliberate red herring. It failed to find the flask and never handled \Character{newcomer}'s wine. Its relationship with \Character{lover} provides a counterweight to its apparent betrayal; it is guilty of conspiracy, but not of this murder.

}
