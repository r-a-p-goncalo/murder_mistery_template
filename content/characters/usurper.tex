\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{Three thousand years ago}, I was a human with an appetite for mysteries that ordinary life could never satisfy. I studied old rites, unusual substances, and every rumour of magic I could find. That is how I met \Character{vampirehead}. She was lonely, powerful, and tired of being the only creature of her kind. I was curious enough to speak to her as an equal.

Together, we developed a potion made with a her blood. A human who drinks it joins the donor's family and gains its gifts and weaknesses. The donor may also set terms for the new vampire. \Character{vampirehead} had no interest in using that power.

I became \Character{vampirehead}'s first companion, and for a thousand years we were close. We travelled, played cruel little games with the world, and enjoyed the freedoms no human could imagine. But I was not satisfied.

My study became the centre of my work. I filled it with notes, rare ingredients, unfinished rituals, and artifacts that no sensible person would leave unattended. I keep many of the notes encrypted. \Character{vampirehead} dislikes the disorder and suspects it hides ambition. She is not wrong, but suspicion is not proof. So I learned to be pleasant, useful, and impossible to dismiss. A helpful hand is rarely examined as closely as a clenched fist.

\timestamp{Two thousand years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} added \Character{enforcer} to the family. She wanted a judge who would make her will into law, and \Character{enforcer} was delighted to have the task. The head gave her the newly created \Clue{rulebook}. The book made the family's laws real: a vampire judged guilty could lose her powers. While the head treated the rules as an administrative detail, I read their consequences carefully. The hierarchy gives the closest vampire a claim to the head's powers if she is stripped of them for a crime. That vampire is me. \Character{vampirehead} created her own succession without noticing it. I did not forget.

\timestamp{Decades after the death of Christ}, \Character{buttler} joined us. She had been a capable merchant, and she remains one at heart. She makes the house run smoothly, keeps accounts, and more.

\timestamp{In 100 AD}, \Character{enforcer} brought \Character{ghoul} into the family. She had fallen in love with a charming jester and did not wish to be separated from him by mortality.

Over the centuries, I watched new vampires arrive, scheme, fail, and sometimes die. When \Character{enforcer} was too busy to carry out a sentence, I was asked to do it. During one such execution, I discovered the secret hidden in the potion I had helped create: a vampire who receives it a second time dies almost at once. I recorded the result among my notes. Knowledge like that should never be wasted.

\timestamp{In 500 AD}, \Character{supremacist} arrived. She was a cruel ruler, yet she treated our family with remarkable respect. \Character{vampirehead} gave her the responsibility of judging whether humans were suitable additions to the family.

\timestamp{In 700 AD}, \Character{lover} joined as the family's protector. He is strong, loyal, and accustomed to solving problems with direct action.

\timestamp{In 1000 AD}, \Character{prevlover} became our investigator. I gave him an artifact that could help expose lies and solve mysteries. I have a hard time remembering details about that artifact.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, \Character{lover} begged \Character{vampirehead} to admit \Character{traitor}. It was a contentious request. \Character{traitor} had spent her human life helping people, which made her an odd fit for our family. The head accepted her for \Character{lover}'s sake. Since then, \Character{traitor} has lectured us about mercy while making common cause with the church. 

I once did an egregious drunken mistake. I told \Character{traitor} in a joke that she could kill herself using the potion that we gave new members. I'm afraid she uses it on \Character{vampirehead}, as that would take my opportunity to inherit her powers. Hopefully she did not take me seriously.

I try to be the family's little helper. I have supplied remedies, repaired small enchantments, and listened kindly when someone needed advice.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, \Character{vampirehead} announced that \Character{newcomer} would join the family at a party at \timestamp{16/7/2026}. Every vampire would have to go.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks before the party}, I offered \Character{vampirehead} if she would like help with the party, I could go two nights before. I knew that, if I told her that, she would tell all the other vampires and ask for the same. If a few people chose that opportunity to steal from my study, they would have their own secrets to explain. A house full of guilty people is a house full of useful distractions. For me, it is harmless, I doubt they could read my notes. Even I can't remember of how they are encrypted.


\timestamp{Two days before the party, at night}, \Character{newcomer} and I went to the castle to help prepare. \Character{vampirehead} and \Character{buttler} were already there.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 12:00}, \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{newcomer}, \Character{buttler}, and I had lunch together. When \Character{buttler} was about to serve the wine, I stopped her and offered a finer bottle of my own for each of them.  \Character{newcomer} drank the potion in that wine and became a vampire without realizing it. \Character{vampirehead} believes she will turn him later. In truth, her correct potion will be his second dose, and it will kill him.

\timestamp{Later yesterday}, maybe because of much I drank, my memory was damaged. It's a shame to be such a light drinker. I can no longer account for every note or artifact that was in my study. That is inconvenient, but it does not change what matters.

\timestamp{This morning, at 11:00}, I saw \Character{lover} and \Character{newcomer} speaking privately outside. Their body language was far too intimate for an innocent conversation.

When \Character{newcomer} dies, grief will make everyone look for the simplest answer. \Character{vampirehead} will have given him the final potion. \Character{supremacist} has always objected to him joining the family. \Character{lover} had a reason to fear losing him. \Character{traitor}'s ideals give her a reason to despise every vampire in this house. I need only be sympathetic and help the family search for not the truth, but a truth.
