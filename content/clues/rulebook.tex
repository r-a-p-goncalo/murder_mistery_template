\paragraph{What it is}

A list of rules.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item An element of the family that has broken a rule will have its powers removed.
    
    \item In case of doubt about the identity of a perpetuator of a crime, a vote shall be made.
    
    \item Elements of the family can't be romantically involved with humans.
        
    \item It is the highest priority crime to murder an element of the family.
    
    \item It is a crime to try to murder an element of the family.
    
    \item It is a crime to steal of elements of the family.
    
    \item It is a crime to not honor promises made to elements of the family.
    
    \item The element of the family with the title of "enforcer" has the power to resolve ambiguities and reduce sentences.
    
    \item If an element of the family loses its powers and responsibilities, the closest element in the hierarchy shall inherit them.
    
    \item The "enforcer" has to make its decisions public.
    
    \item If the "enforcer" is the criminal in question, it will lose its powers for that situation. \Character{vampirehead} shall control the situation.
    
    \item In case of various crimes interconnected, only the highest priority crime shall be processed.
\end{enumerate}
