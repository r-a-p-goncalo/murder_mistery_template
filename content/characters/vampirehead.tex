\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{Thousands of years ago}, I was human and desperate to escape every human weakness: hunger, pain, and dependence on other people. Something heard that desire and responded. I became unimaginably powerful, bending all there is to my will. As a result, the world hated me, and I hated it back. Its sun continuously tried to banish me, and I took revenge by drinking the blood of its children. I had become the first vampire. A god. I could turn my thoughts into rules by nothing but my will. It was so long ago that I barely think of those times anymore.

Time passed, and brought loneliness.

\timestamp{Three thousand years ago}, I met an amazing little human. Without any curse or blessing, her curiosity had led her to study the mysteries of magic. I took a liking to that human and wanted to share my blessing with her. Together, we developed a potion derived from my blood, which allowed me to take willing humans into my family. Theoretically, I could define rules at the moment someone drank my blood, and that vampire would have to follow them. But I did not care for that.

Time passed, and brought joy. \timestamp{For a thousand years} we had fun, doing things that no mortal could dream.

But all things end. And so, \Character{usurper}'s interest in the mystic arts gradually took over her character. At some point, research filled \Character{usurper}'s heart. And space. There was always a mess of artifacts and notes, all encrypted so no mere mortal could stumble across them. I did not particularly care. I just hated the mess. I wondered whether \Character{usurper}'s greed for power came from something other than obsession. Would having my level of control over nature be enough, or even if she gained as much power as I had, would the search for more still continue?

I wanted to expand the family, but did not really want the responsibilities that might come with it. I decided that, if I expanded the family, I would divide responsibilities among its members.

\timestamp{A little over 2 thousand years ago}, I introduced \Character{enforcer} to the family. I wanted a judge, someone to enforce rules. \Character{enforcer} fit that role. She loved justice and control. So, I created a book, my version of the law, our bible. I gave the book to \Character{enforcer}, who would be responsible for knowing and enforcing its contents. Maybe because it does not concern me, I do not really remember what rules I wrote.

\timestamp{Some decades after the death of Christ}, I was ready to add a new element to the family. I somewhat admired merchants and also wanted some kind of servant. If I wanted to expand the family, I wanted someone who would help me in the castle. So, I introduced \Character{buttler} to our circle, a famous merchant at the time. She would make sure that our house was clean, oversee our finances, and satisfy her greed however she wanted.

\timestamp{In 100 AD}, \Character{enforcer} asked me to introduce \Character{ghoul}, a jester, to the family. \Character{enforcer} had an additional request: the potion given to \Character{ghoul} should contain her blood. She told me it was so they could be closer, as they were in love. I allowed it, and they did it in private. This was unusual, as we had held a small party when \Character{enforcer} and \Character{buttler} drank the potion. To keep appearances, we still held the party, but the potion I gave \Character{ghoul} was a fake.

Later, \Character{buttler} asked me whether the potion for \Character{ghoul} was real. I simply paid her for her silence. I did not want other vampires to get the same idea.

The years went by. I added a lot of new elements to the family, and it always ended in tragedy. They either tried to take over the world, were especially psychotic, or even tried to kill me. \Character{enforcer} was occupied enforcing the rules and stripping offenders of their powers. Sometimes, \Character{usurper} had to be responsible for executions.

\timestamp{In 500 AD}, we met \Character{supremacist}, a terrible dictator who believed in her supremacy above all else. Weirdly, when we interacted with \Character{supremacist}, our family was always treated well. Because she was so selective, I decided to add her to our family, giving her the responsibility to evaluate whether a new member would be appropriate for the family.

\timestamp{In 700 AD}, we met \Character{lover}, a wandering warrior. We decided it would be useful to have someone responsible for military operations, and so he was added.

\timestamp{In 1000 AD}, we met \Character{prevlover}, a so-called "investigator." Having someone responsible for an intelligence network seemed like a good idea, and so he was added.

\timestamp{In 1400 AD}, \Character{lover} presented \Character{traitor} as a possible addition to the family. It was strange, as \Character{traitor} was known as something of a martyr for the people, not really in line with our family's values. \Character{supremacist} did not want her there, but \Character{lover} insisted so much that I accepted. The argument was that having someone pushing our moral compass in a good direction would prevent us from ever turning into true monsters.

The addition of \Character{traitor} ended up being a mistake. She never stops talking about how much we could help and so on.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, I met \Character{newcomer}, an amazing human. I had seen love hundreds of times, but never felt it. Since the moment I set eyes on him, I knew I had to add him to the family. Because of my own rules, I could not have a romantic relationship with him until he was added to the family. At least, that is the reason I think I did not immediately begin a relationship with him. I do not really remember the rules well.

I invited him to a party of our family and asked \Character{supremacist} for her opinion. She was adamant that he was not suitable for the family, to the point that I had to threaten to remove her from her responsibilities.

I wanted some time before adding \Character{newcomer} to the family. Maybe \Character{supremacist} was right. In that time, I tried seeing him less, but my opinion and feelings did not change.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, In the usual fashion, I marked \timestamp{16/7/2026} as the date of the party to add \Character{newcomer} to the family, and all vampires, as was usual in the family, had to come.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks before the party}, \Character{usurper} asked me if I would like help preparing it, as \Character{buttler} could not be enough. I appreciated the offer and extended the invitation to all the family and \Character{newcomer}. Only \Character{newcomer} accepted.

\timestamp{The day before the party}, when the help was due, \Character{usurper}, \Character{buttler}, \Character{newcomer}, and I had lunch together. All seemed well. \Character{usurper} was helpful as always, bringing food and drinks tailored for each of us. When \Character{buttler} was about to serve the wine, \Character{usurper} stopped her, saying she had brought her own higher-quality wine instead. I did not think twice about it.

\timestamp{At 21:00}, \Character{newcomer} goes outside without telling me, and disappears for the night.

\timestamp{In the morning today (16/7/2026),} I confront \Character{newcomer}, but he only dodges the questions and jokes. I do not know. Maybe I am being paranoid.

There is much work to do. Food to prepare. Decorations. After I give \Character{newcomer} the potion, we will be able to be together.

\GMNotes{

\Character{vampirehead} uses the correct ritual and does not know about the first dose. Her concealed love for \Character{newcomer}, her authority over the rules, and her past payment to \Character{buttler} all make her a convincing suspect. Once the double-dose effect is established, make clear that the head was deceived and that the person who arranged the lunch wine is responsible.


\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{vampirehead} is the apparent killer because she physically gives \Character{newcomer} the second potion.

There is also a possibility that someone might question whether she found out about \Character{newcomer}'s affairs.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

It is a weak theory that \Character{vampirehead} killed \Character{newcomer}. Why would she do it today, in front of everyone? Why even hold the party to turn \Character{newcomer} if the objective was to kill him?

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story?}

\Character{vampirehead} is the character with the most insight into the history of the family, and may disprove many theories.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

\Character{vampirehead} is actually the only one whose background gives her a good chance of deducing \Character{usurper}'s motive, and possibly how the killing happened. It greatly reduces the number of clues needed.
