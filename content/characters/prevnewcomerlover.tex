\shortdescription{The family's personal investigator.}

\ObjectivesHeading{Objectives}

  \item Find \Character{newcomer}'s murderer and see the murderer voted out. (\PositiveReward{1}, \NegativeReward{1})

\LosingConditionsHeading{Losing conditions}
Fail to vote out \Character{newcomer}'s murderer, if there is one.

\SecretsHeading{Secrets}
\begin{itemize}
  \item I had a secret relationship with \Character{newcomer}. (\NegativeReward{1}, \PositiveReward{1})

  \item I used the stolen ritual on \Character{enforcer}'s memory when she threatened to expose my relationship with \Character{newcomer}. (\NegativeReward{1}, \PositiveReward{1})
\end{itemize}

\ActionsHeading{Actions}
\begin{itemize}
  \item Treat every claim as a lead. Ask who saw it, when it happened, and what evidence remains.
\end{itemize}

\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{In 1000 AD}, I joined the family because I was good at noticing what other people preferred to hide. I'm not one to boast, but I solved crimes in at least 1 village. \Character{vampirehead} wanted an investigator, and so she chose me. I was filled with excitement to become a supernatural being, and I loved the party they threw me. \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{usurper}, \Character{enforcer}, \Character{buttler}, \Character{ghoul}, \Character{supremacist} and \Character{lover} were surprisingly good company.
 
\Character{usurper} gave me a small artifact that helped distinguish truth from falsehood in written statements. I can't really remember the details about the artifact, besides the it only works once a day.
 
\Character{enforcer} and I worked together often, even if she did not seem specially found of me, she found me useful.

\Character{traitor} arrived in \timestamp{1400 AD} after \Character{lover} pleaded for her admission. I and all the others considered her kindness an odd fit for this household.

\timestamp{Two years ago}, \Character{vampirehead} introduced \Character{newcomer} at a family gathering. The head's interest in him was obvious, and so was the danger of becoming close to him. I did it anyway. We began a relationship in secret. For a while, I thought I had found someone who could make an immortal life feel new again.

\timestamp{A year ago}, \Character{newcomer} ended that relationship. He said, \"What we are doing is wrong\", and gave me nothing more. I assumed the head's interest had frightened him.

Around that time, \Character{enforcer} asked me to help investigate a missing page from the \Clue{rulebook}. She suspected \Character{traitor}, partly because of her hostility to the family. We intercepted unusual correspondence connected to \Character{traitor}'s and \Character{lover}'s homes. The messages seemed important, but the investigation produced more loose ends than conclusions. I kept the notes, along with my artifact, in my bag. What was written on them? I wish I could remember. I swear my memory is usually much better.

\timestamp{Two months ago}, \Character{vampirehead} announced that \Character{newcomer} would join the family at a party on \timestamp{16/7/2026}, and every vampire was required to attend.

\timestamp{A couple of weeks before the party}, \Character{vampirehead} asked whether anyone besides \Character{usurper}, \Character{buttler}, and \Character{newcomer} wished to arrive two nights early and help. I declined. The invitation made it clear that \Character{usurper}'s study would be unattended on the morning before the party.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 01:00}, I entered \Character{usurper}'s study looking for a way to understand \Character{newcomer} and perhaps make him love me again. I found a ritual called \"To change and know a person\". It promised access to a person's memories and the power to alter them. I stole it. Someone saw me in the study. In my hurry to leave, I dropped my bag, including the truth-seeing artifact and the correspondence notes. I have not recovered any of it.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 16:00}, \Character{enforcer} called me. She said she would overlook the theft if I surrendered the ritual. She wanted to use it on \Character{newcomer} because someone had warned her that he would die. I realized that looking through his memories would expose our relationship, and the family would have grounds to take my powers. I panicked.

\timestamp{At 16:15}, I used the ritual on \Character{enforcer}'s memory before she could use it on \Character{newcomer}'s. I meant to make her forget the agreement, but I accelerated the ritual and something went wrong. I know, because this was the exact moment I started having problems with my memory also. It seems like I affected us both. The ritual produced leftovers

\timestamp{At 17:00}, the leftovers of the ritual disappeared without me noticing. Where could they be?


\GMNotes{

\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{prevlover} had a painful romantic motive, entered \Character{usurper}'s study without permission, stole powerful memory magic, and deliberately erased \Character{enforcer}'s memory. The missing artifact and intercepted-message notes can make it look as though he destroyed or concealed evidence as well.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

\Character{prevlover}'s wrongdoing occurred at \timestamp{16:15}, more than four hours after \Character{newcomer} received \Character{usurper}'s wine at lunch. The memory ritual caused confusion and concealed evidence, but it did not place the first dose in the wine. \Character{enforcer}'s call and \Character{ghoul}'s earlier warning establish that \Character{prevlover} was reacting to a death that had already been set in motion.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

\Character{prevlover} is both a compromised investigator and a route to several physical clues. His dropped bag explains why the truth-seeing artifact and the correspondence records can be discovered away from their owner. His knowledge of \Character{enforcer}'s attempted intervention makes the memory ritual a meaningful crime without making it the murder.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

Once he admits what \Character{enforcer} said on the call, the group has a reason to establish when the death was first predicted. \Character{ghoul} can then place the warning at \timestamp{15:00}, after the lunch that \Character{usurper} attended. That ordering rules out the later rituals as the cause and sends the investigation back to the wine substitution at lunch.

}
