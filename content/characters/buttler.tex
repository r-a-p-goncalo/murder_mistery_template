\BackgroundHeading{Background}

\timestamp{Decades after the death of Christ}, I was a merchant with a reputation for keeping clean books and making difficult arrangements seem effortless. \Character{vampirehead} invited me into her family because she needed my management skills. I accepted because immortality is the finest investment I have ever made. I got a party with only \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{enforcer} and \Character{usurper}. It was horrible, they truly needed me.

Since then, I have kept this castle supplied, orderly, and solvent.

\timestamp{In 100 AD}, a new member was introduced, \Character{ghoul}, an entertainer of sorts. He was presented by \Character{enforcer}, which seemed to desire a deeper relationship with him. At the party I did notice that the forbidden potion \Character{vampirehead} offered seemed to have a different hue. When I asked her, she offered me money for my silence, and so I did not press the subject further. 

\timestamp{A couple of weeks ago}, \Character{vampirehead} announced that \Character{newcomer} would join the family at tonight's party. I began preparing the house at once. \Character{usurper} offered to help, and \Character{newcomer} joined us the day before the gathering. It was an unusually small working party for such an important occasion, which should have made me suspicious.

\timestamp{Yesterday morning}, the cellar inventory and my accounts both acquired details I cannot explain. There is an unsigned payment, and a bottle had been set aside for \Character{newcomer}, but the visitor, the request, and the purpose of the payment have vanished from my mind. I hate an incomplete ledger.

\timestamp{Yesterday, at 12:00}, I joined \Character{vampirehead}, \Character{newcomer}, and \Character{usurper} for lunch. I was about to serve the wine intended for \Character{newcomer} when \Character{usurper} intervened. She said she had brought a better bottle and made a small performance of selecting it especially for him. \Character{newcomer} drank from her wine. I remember that moment perfectly because it was not a transaction. It was simply odd.

\timestamp{Later yesterday}, something disturbed more than the cellar. The memories attached to every older payment I had accepted became ragged at once. I still possess the coins and my careful records, but not the identities or favours behind them. Whoever meddled with my memory did not understand bookkeeping: an unexplained entry is not erased, only made conspicuous.

\timestamp{This morning, at 11:00}, I saw \Character{lover} and \Character{newcomer} speaking in private outside. Their farewell had all the tenderness and panic of a secret about to become expensive. Once \Character{newcomer} left, I offered \Character{lover} my discretion. He paid without much bargaining, so the secret must matter to him. Unlike the older payments, I remember every detail of this one.

\GMNotes{

\paragraph{Why would someone suspect this character?}

\Character{buttler} manages the house and handled the service at lunch. She has a known habit of selling silence, possesses unexplained payments, and now holds a fresh payment from \Character{lover}. Those facts make it easy to imagine her concealing a crime or helping someone else conceal one.

\paragraph{How can innocence of this character be proved?}

The lunch timeline clears \Character{buttler} of arranging the fatal dose. She was preparing to serve the wine already set aside when \Character{usurper} publicly replaced it with her own bottle for \Character{newcomer}. \Character{buttler} did not choose that bottle or know what it contained. Her memory gaps concern older bribes, while the later wine substitution remains a clear, consistent observation that other witnesses can confirm.

\paragraph{What is this character's role in the story and game?}

\Character{buttler} is a practical, negotiable witness. She turns domestic details into leads: which bottle was meant to be served, who changed the plan, which guest was where, and which private conversation might supply a motive. Her instinct to charge for information gives players a reason to bargain, threaten, or earn her trust instead of receiving every fact immediately.

\paragraph{How can this character point into the intended solution?}

If questioned about lunch, \Character{buttler} can establish the decisive sequence: a different wine had been prepared for \Character{newcomer}, then \Character{usurper} substituted her own supposedly superior bottle and \Character{newcomer} drank it. Combined with the evidence that a second turning dose kills a vampire, that account directs the investigation to \Character{usurper}'s wine as the first dose and to her as the murderer.
