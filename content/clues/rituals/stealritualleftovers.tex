

\ClueSheet{clue:stealritualleftovers}{Stealing ritual leftovers}{

\paragraph{What it is}

Ritual leftovers with the symbol of stealing. When solved, it shows a vision of \Character{traitor}, and then of \Character{usurper}'s study and the items in it, with a focus on potions.


\paragraph{How can it be found / solved}

The first player to correctly point out what the symbol is, receives visions, given by the GM.

\paragraph{What it reveals}

Points to \Character{traitor} trying to steal from \Character{usurper} a way of killing the vampires.

All the ritual clues, when discovered, motivate the affected characters to reveal what happened in \Character{usurper}'s study.
